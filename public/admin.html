System Handover & Disclaimer

Project: MLA Appointment Management System

1. Purpose of This Document

This document formally records the handover of the MLA Appointment Management System ("the System") from the developer to the designated administrator(s) of the MLA's office. It defines the scope of the developer's responsibility and clarifies that all operational responsibility transfers to the administrator(s) upon handover.

2. Scope of Developer's Responsibility

The developer's role was limited to the following:

  • Design, development, and testing of the System
  • Initial deployment and configuration
  • Providing necessary documentation for system usage and maintenance

The developer's responsibility ends at the point of successful handover and deployment confirmation.

3. Transfer of Operational Control

Upon handover, the following are transferred fully to the administrator(s):

  • Admin panel credentials and access control
  • Ownership and management of citizen appointment data
  • Day-to-day operation, monitoring, and maintenance of the System
  • Responsibility for safeguarding login credentials and system access

4. Disclaimer of Liability

From the date of handover onward:

  • The developer holds no ongoing responsibility or liability for the System's operation, data handling, or security.
  • Any incidents including, but not limited to, unauthorized access, hacking, data breaches, data loss, misuse of citizen information, system downtime, or misconfiguration occurring after handover are the sole responsibility of the administrator(s) managing the System.
  • The developer shall not be held liable in any legal, financial, administrative, or reputational capacity for any consequences arising from the System's use, misuse, or compromise after the handover date.
  • This includes incidents caused by weak password practices, failure to update credentials, mishandling of admin access, or any other administrative negligence occurring post-handover.

5. Administrator's Acknowledged Responsibilities

By accepting handover of this System, the administrator(s) acknowledge responsibility for:

  • Maintaining confidentiality of admin login credentials
  • Ensuring only authorized personnel access the admin panel
  • Reporting and addressing any security concerns independently or through appropriately contracted support
  • Complying with applicable data protection norms in managing citizen information

6. No Warranty

The System is provided "as-is" at the time of handover. No warranty, express or implied, is made regarding uninterrupted operation, absolute security, or fitness for any particular purpose beyond what was demonstrated and agreed upon during development.
